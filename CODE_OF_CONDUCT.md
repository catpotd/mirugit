# Code of Conduct

## The standard

Everyone taking part here — issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions — is
expected to behave the way you would want a colleague to behave.

That means:

- Argue about the code, not the person who wrote it
- Say what is wrong and what would be right; "this is bad" helps no one
- Take a correction as information, not as an attack
- Assume the other person is trying to make the project better

That rules out:

- Insults, slurs, and demeaning comments about anyone's identity or background
- Sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Publishing anyone's private information
- Sustained disruption of a discussion, or deliberately wasting people's time

## Reporting

Open a report on
[the Security tab](https://github.com/catpotd/mirugit/security/advisories/new)
if the behaviour involves private information you do not want in public.
Otherwise, contact the maintainers by opening an issue that names what happened
and links to it.

Reports are handled as maintainer availability allows; no response time is
guaranteed.

## What happens next

The maintainers decide, and say what they decided and why. The range is a
private correction, a public one, deleting the comment, and blocking the
account from the repository. A maintainer who breaks this rule is held to it in
the same way.
